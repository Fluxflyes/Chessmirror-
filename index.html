How to Read the Model
Doc
Each detector tracks occurrence, opportunity and choice independently. An opportunity is position-level: at least one legal move satisfies the same detector. Objective nonsense is filtered softly rather than by popularity; unusual but playable choices stay in the sample. A player's own poor move is not a decline, and reliability is kept separate from the fingerprint score.
